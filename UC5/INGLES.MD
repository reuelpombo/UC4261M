por exemplo:
**simple present** she drinks coffe in the morning 
**present continuos** She is drinking coffe now.


1. **Simple Present:** She drinks coffee in the morning.
   **Present Continuous:** She is drinking coffee now.
   **Português:** Ela está tomando café agora.

2. **Simple Present:** They run in the park.
   **Present Continuous:** They are running in the park now.
   **Português:** Eles estão correndo no parque agora.

3. **Simple Present:** He drives to work.
   **Present Continuous:** He is driving to work now.
   **Português:** Ele está dirigindo para o trabalho agora.

4. **Simple Present:** I listen to music.
   **Present Continuous:** I am listening to music now.
   **Português:** Eu estou ouvindo música agora.

5. **Simple Present:** We make dinner together.
   **Present Continuous:** We are making dinner together now.
   **Português:** Nós estamos fazendo o jantar juntos agora.

6. **Simple Present:** John swims in the pool.
   **Present Continuous:** John is swimming in the pool now.
   **Português:** John está nadando na piscina agora.

7. **Simple Present:** My mom buys vegetables at the market.
   **Present Continuous:** My mom is buying vegetables at the market now.
   **Português:** Minha mãe está comprando vegetais no mercado agora.

8. **Simple Present:** The students learn English.
   **Present Continuous:** The students are learning English now.
   **Português:** Os estudantes estão aprendendo inglês agora.

   **Present Continuous:** Anna is taking pictures of her friends now.
